D. On the Importance of Perception-Aware Flight
   Weconductanablationstudy toinvestigatetheimportance
of  being  perception-aware  in  vision-based  ﬂight.  We  use
the Racing MW environment for this study due to its high
complexity.  TableIII  shows  the  results.  When  ﬂying  non-
perception-aware, the vehicle achieves faster lap time since
the policy only maximizes its progress along the path. When
ﬂying perception-aware, the policy achieves higher lap time
sinceithastotrade-offbetweenmaximizingtheprogressand
optimizing the visibility of the next ﬂight direction. Despite
being able to ﬂy along a faster trajectory, the non-perception-
aware vision-based policy achieves a lower success rate. This
is because the camera orientation is not well-aligned with
the ﬂight direction; as a result, the policy cannot react to
upcoming obstacles in time.
